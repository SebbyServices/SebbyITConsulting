About a charge

Saw SEBBYSERVICES.COM on your statement?

That charge is for expert tech support you received through a JustAnswer membership. Sebby IT Consulting, Corp. is an independent expert on JustAnswer, and JustAnswer bills its membership under the name of the expert who helped you.

Questions

Common questions

Why does my statement say SEBBYSERVICES.COM and not JustAnswer?

JustAnswer bills membership charges under the name of the independent expert assigned to help you. Sebby IT Consulting, Corp. is that expert’s business.

I don’t remember signing up.

JustAnswer usually starts a monthly membership when you pay to ask an expert a question. If you don’t recognize it or want to cancel, call JustAnswer Customer Care at 1-800-337-9589. They can review the charge with you.

Can Sebby IT give me a refund?

No. Refunds and cancellations for JustAnswer memberships are handled only by JustAnswer. Please contact their Customer Care team above.

Is this charge related to a Sebby IT support plan?

Only if you signed up for Sebby IT Shield or Sebby IT Care directly with us. Those charges are billed separately and we’ll gladly help with them on our contact page.
