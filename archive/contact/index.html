Frequently Asked

What happens in the free audit?

We spend 30 minutes understanding your business, tech stack, and biggest challenges. Then we give you honest feedback on security risks, growth opportunities, and a path forward. No sales pitch. No long-term commitment.

What's your typical timeline?

Depends on scope. Small projects (landing page, API integration) take 1-2 weeks. Medium projects (WordPress site, automation pipeline) take 3-6 weeks. Enterprise work is built in phases. We always front-load discovery.

Do you offer retainers?

Yes. After project delivery, many clients keep us on retainer for ongoing support, maintenance, and scaled growth (hosting, monitoring, feature additions). $500-$5K/month depending on scope.

What if I'm not sure what I need?

Perfect. That's what the free audit is for. Bring your challenge. We'll sketch out options, estimate costs, and recommend the highest-ROI path. Many clients start here and end up doing 3-5 phases together.
