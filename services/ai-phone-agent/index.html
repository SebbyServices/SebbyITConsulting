What it does

Never miss another customer call

Every missed call is a customer who may call your competitor next. Your AI phone agent picks up every time.

Answers every call

Nights, weekends, and busy hours. No more missed calls going to a full voicemail box.

Books appointments

Schedules, confirms, and reschedules on your calendar while the caller is still on the line.

Handles common questions

Hours, location, services, and pricing, answered the same way every time.

English, Spanish, or both

Set up to match your customers, so every caller can use the language they’re most comfortable in.

Sends you a summary

A text or email recap after each call: who called, what they needed, and any follow-up.

Transfers urgent calls

When a caller needs a real person, the agent routes the call to you or your team.

Whichever your customers need. We decide together during discovery: English only, Spanish only, or fully bilingual.

Can I keep my current phone number?

In most cases, yes. We confirm the best way to connect your existing line during discovery.

Is there a contract?

The $500/mo fee has a 6-month minimum. After that, it continues month to month.

What if a caller needs a real person?

The agent can transfer the call to you or your team, or take a detailed message and send it to you right away.
