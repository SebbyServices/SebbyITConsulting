Questions

Common questions

What counts as phone or screen-share time?

Any call or screen-share where we are actively working on your issue. Chat and email questions are always unlimited and never count against your hours.

What if we need more hours than the plan includes?

Additional phone or screen-share time is billed at $100/hr.

Do you come on-site?

Yes, in the Miami area and the Dominican Republic. On-site visits are billed separately from the monthly plan.

Why is a cybersecurity background useful for a small business?

Because most small business IT problems are also security problems: shared passwords, old devices, phishing emails. The monthly check-ins and quarterly audits are there to catch those early.
