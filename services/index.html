What’s covered

What we fix

Every plan and one-time fix covers these four areas.

Computers & devices

Your computer should work for you, not against you. We diagnose and fix laptops, desktops, phones, and tablets.

  • Slow performance and crashes
  • Virus and malware removal
  • Hardware diagnostics and upgrades (RAM, SSD)
  • Backups and data recovery
  • New computer or phone setup and data transfer

WiFi & networks

Dropped connections and dead zones are frustrating. We set up and fix home and small office networks.

  • WiFi setup and dead-zone fixes
  • Router and modem configuration
  • Internet speed troubleshooting
  • Printer and smart device connections
  • Basic network security

Email, apps & accounts

We handle the setup and the fixes so you can get back to what you were doing.

  • Email setup: Gmail, Outlook, iCloud
  • Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace
  • Software installs and updates
  • Password and account recovery help
  • Cloud storage and backups

Scam safety & coaching

Patient, one-on-one help to build confidence with your devices, at your own pace, in English or Spanish.

  • Spotting scam calls, texts, and pop-ups
  • Safer passwords and account security
  • Smartphone and tablet lessons
  • Email, video calls, and online basics
  • Help for parents and grandparents

Questions

Pricing questions

What counts as a remote session?

Any phone call, video call, or screen-share where we are actively working on your issue together. Chat, text, and email questions never count against it.

Do I have to sign up for a plan?

No. If you only have one problem, a one-time fix is a flat $95–125 depending on complexity, and we agree on the price before we start.

Can you come on-site?

Yes, in the Miami area and the Dominican Republic. On-site visits are billed separately from the monthly plans.

Do you help in Spanish?

Yes. Every plan is fully bilingual. Write to us in whichever language you prefer.
