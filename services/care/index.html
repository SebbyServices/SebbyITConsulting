Scam safety

Scammers target parents and grandparents first.

Fake Microsoft alerts. Phony IRS calls. Gift card requests. “Your computer has a virus” pop-ups that are the real threat.

The Family plan includes a monthly check-in where we go through anything suspicious your parent or grandparent ran into, and set up protections on their devices that block the most common tricks.

What we check every month

  • Suspicious emails, texts, or links they received
  • Pop-ups and “warning” messages
  • Calls from unknown “tech support”
  • Any app they were asked to install
  • Password and account security

Questions

Common questions

I’m not good with technology. Is that okay?

Absolutely. That’s exactly who this is for. We explain everything in plain language and never make you feel bad for asking.

Can my parents use the plan if they live somewhere else?

Yes. Support is remote first, so the Family plan works wherever your family members live. On-site help is available in the Miami area and the Dominican Republic.

What counts as a remote session?

A phone call, video call, or screen-share where we work on the problem together. Chat and email quick-fixes are unlimited and don’t count.

Do you help in Spanish?

Sí. Every plan is fully bilingual.
