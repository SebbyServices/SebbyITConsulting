Common Questions

Got questions? I've got answers.

How quickly will you respond to my inquiry? ▼

I aim to get back to you within 1-2 business days. Since I work with each client personally, I make sure to give your inquiry the attention it deserves.

How long will my project take? ▼

Timeline depends entirely on the scope of your project. A simple website tweak might take days, while a full content production pipeline could take weeks. During our initial consultation, I'll give you a realistic estimate based on what you need.

Do you work remotely or locally in Miami? ▼

I work both ways. Right now, I'm primarily taking on remote clients, but I'm available for local projects in the Miami area when needed. Either way, you get the same level of personal attention and quality work.

How is pricing structured? ▼

Pricing depends on the specific service and scope of work. Some projects are flat-rate, others are hourly. The best way to find out what your project will cost is to reach out and tell me what you're working on — I'll give you a clear, no-surprise quote.
