The Problem

ThePatientChase was shipping 75–150 orders per drop, but their shipping process was a major bottleneck consuming 8–12 hours per drop:

  • Manual address writing on every polymailer
  • Post office dependency — $6–8 per label charged individually
  • Low frequency — only 1–2 drops/month due to time investment
  • No tracking — customers had zero visibility
  • Chargebacks & disputes — lack of transparency

The Solution

Integrated Shipping Automation

We built a complete automation system connecting Wix orders to Shippo's bulk-rate API, with direct integration to a label printer. Orders flow from customer order → API call → label generated → printer ready → customer notified.

Timeline: 2 weeks from discovery to first automated drop.

By eliminating manual processes and automating the shipping workflow, ThePatientChase transformed an 8–12 hour bottleneck into a streamlined, repeatable process.
